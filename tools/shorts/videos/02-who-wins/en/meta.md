# Description

Four duels for the same money, and it comes full circle: tanks beat infantry, drones beat tanks, rocketeers beat drones, and infantry beats rocketeers. In Kharos no unit beats everything: whoever picks the right army wins.

Every result is a real in-game battle, not staged.
